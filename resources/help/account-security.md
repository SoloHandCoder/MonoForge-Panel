# Account security

Your **Account** page holds your email, password, two-factor authentication and linked accounts.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from your phone each time you sign in, so a stolen password is not enough.

1. On your Account page, start setting up two-step verification.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app of your choice.
3. Enter the 6-digit code it shows and your password, then confirm.
4. Save the **recovery codes** you are shown somewhere safe. They are shown only once.

If you lose your phone, sign in with one of the recovery codes instead of a code from the app.

Your host may require 2FA for every account; the panel then asks you to set it up before you continue.

## Linked accounts

If your host turned on Discord or Google sign-in, your Account page lists them.

- **Link** connects your Discord or Google account, so you can sign in with it.
- **Unlink** removes it; you can no longer sign in with that account.
- If you created your account with Discord or Google, set a password first, so you can still sign in after unlinking your last linked account.

## Good habits

- Use a password you do not use anywhere else.
- Never share your password, recovery codes or API keys. Our team will never ask for them.
- To let someone help with a server, invite them as a user instead of sharing your account. See [Friends and sharing access](/help/friends-and-sharing-access).
