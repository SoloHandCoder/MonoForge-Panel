# Getting started

Welcome to MonoForge! This guide takes you from a new account to your first server.

## Create an account

1. Open the panel and choose **Create an account** on the sign-in page.
2. Pick a **username**, enter your **email** and choose a **password** of at least 8 characters.
3. Tick the box to agree to the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), then create the account.

## Sign in with Discord or Google

If your host turned them on, the sign-in page shows **Continue with Discord** and **Continue with Google** buttons.

- The first time you use one, MonoForge creates your account from it. You see a short screen that names the Discord or Google account you are signing up with, where you agree to the terms before the account is made.
- Already have an account? Link Discord or Google to it on your **Account** page and use either button to sign in from then on. See [Account security](/help/account-security).

## The dashboard

After signing in you land on the dashboard:

- **Servers** lists every server you own or were invited to. Each card shows whether the server is online, its CPU, memory and disk use, and the address players join on. Hover over a card for quick actions such as opening the console.
- **Store** is where you buy a new server or start a free trial. See [Buying a server](/help/buying-a-server).
- **Wallet** shows your token balance and lets you buy more. See [Tokens and the Wallet](/help/tokens-and-wallet).
- **Account** holds your password, two-factor authentication, linked accounts, friends and support tickets.

Open a server to reach its console, files, plugins, backups, schedules and settings in the sidebar.
