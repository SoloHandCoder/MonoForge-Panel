# Tokens and the Wallet

Tokens are the panel's currency. You buy them in packs, then spend them on servers: each server takes its price in tokens once per billing period, automatically.

**Tokens never expire.** Whatever you do not spend stays in your wallet.

## Your wallet

Open **Wallet** in the sidebar to see:

- your **balance**;
- the **token packs** on sale;
- **Redeem a coupon**, for free-tokens coupons;
- your **token history**: every purchase, server charge and refund.

## Buying a token pack

1. On the Wallet page, choose **Buy** on a pack.
2. You are taken to the checkout of **Tebex**, our payment partner. Pay there.
3. Tebex sends you back to your wallet, which shows the result of the payment.

By buying you agree to the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds).

## The return screen

When you come back from Tebex, the wallet tells you where your payment stands:

- **Confirming** – Tebex is confirming the payment. This usually takes a few seconds.
- **Tokens added** – the tokens are in your wallet, with your new balance. From here you can go to the store.
- **Still confirming** – after a minute without an answer. Your tokens are added automatically once Tebex confirms, so you can close the page.
- **Payment being checked** – Tebex received the payment, but our team checks it before the tokens are added. You get a notice when it is done.
- **Payment declined** or **Payment refunded** – no tokens were added.
- **Checkout cancelled** – you left the checkout before paying. If you did pay, the tokens still arrive once Tebex confirms.

## Coupons

- A **free-tokens coupon** goes in **Redeem a coupon** on the Wallet page and adds its tokens straight away.
- A **discount coupon** goes in at the store checkout instead. See [Buying a server](/help/buying-a-server).

## Refunds

Token purchases follow our [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds). Spent tokens and running billing periods are not refunded.
