# Buying a server

Open **Store** in the sidebar. Plans are grouped by category, cheapest first. Each card shows the plan's resources, the games it runs and its lowest monthly price. A plan that is cheaper over a longer period says **Save up to …% with a longer period**.

## Checking out

Choose a plan, then go through the checkout:

1. **Choose your game.** The server comes set up for it.
2. **Pick a location.** Some locations cost a little more.
3. **Billing period.** How often the server renews, such as every 30 days. Longer periods can be cheaper per day.
4. **Name your server.** You can rename it later. If your host offers custom subdomains, you can also pick one here.
5. **Add-ons.** Optional extras such as more memory, disk, CPU, backups, databases or ports. They are billed with the server, every period.

The order summary on the side adds it all up. Confirm, and the panel reserves the resources, charges your tokens and creates the server, usually in under a minute. The game is installed for you, so you only have to press **Start**.

By buying you agree to the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds).

## Coupons

Have a discount code? Choose **Have a coupon?** in the order summary and enter it. The summary shows the discount before you buy.

- A discount applies to the **first period**. Renewals are full price.
- A coupon that does not apply to this order says why; remove it to buy without it.
- Coupons that give free tokens are redeemed on the Wallet page instead. See [Tokens and the Wallet](/help/tokens-and-wallet).

## Free trials

Some plans can be tried for free: their card shows **Try free**. A trial charges nothing and lasts for the number of hours its checkout shows.

- There is **one free trial per player**, and trials are for players who have not bought a server from the store yet.
- Your host may ask you to link a Discord or Google account first, or to pass a captcha.
- A trial server has no add-ons and no billing period until you keep it.

### Keep this server

While the trial runs, every page of the server shows a banner with the time left. To keep the server with everything on it:

1. Pick a billing period in the banner.
2. Choose **Keep this server**. Its price is charged from your wallet; if you are short, choose **Top up** first.

The server then becomes a normal server that renews every period. If you do not keep it, it is suspended when the trial ends and deleted after the grace period; the banner counts down to that, and **Keep this server** still brings it back until then.

## After buying

Your server renews automatically from your token balance. Read [Renewals and unpaid servers](/help/renewals-and-unpaid-servers) to see what happens when your balance runs low.
