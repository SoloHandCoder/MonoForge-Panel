# Support tickets

Something wrong with a server, your account or a payment? Open a ticket and our team will take a look. Open **Support** in your account to see your tickets.

## Opening a ticket

Choose **New ticket** and fill in:

- **Subject:** a short summary, such as "Server stops after loading the world".
- **Category:** what the ticket is about.
- **Server:** the server it is about, or **Not about a server**.
- **Message:** what happened and what you already tried. Markdown works, and you can attach files such as screenshots.

The more you tell us up front, such as the time it happened and the error in the console, the faster we can help.

## Replies

We answer in the ticket and email you when we do. Tickets with a new reply are marked in your list.

## Closing a ticket

Close the ticket when your problem is solved. Replying within 7 days opens it again; after that, open a new ticket if you still need help.

For quick questions you can also [join our Discord](https://discord.gg/B3fd45pgvC).
