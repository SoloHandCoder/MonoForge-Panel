# Renewals and unpaid servers

A server bought in the store renews once per billing period, paid from your token balance. Its console shows the price, the period and the next renewal date.

## Auto-renew

**Auto-renew** is on for every new server, so the renewal is paid automatically. You can switch it off on the server's console:

- With auto-renew **off**, the server is suspended on its renewal date unless you renew it first (see below).
- **Renew now** pays the next period ahead of time. You can pay at most one period ahead.

## Low balance warnings

A few days before a renewal your balance cannot cover, you get a **Balance too low for a renewal** notice in the panel and an email. Top up in your [wallet](/help/tokens-and-wallet) before the date and nothing else happens.

## Suspension and the grace period

If a renewal cannot be paid (or auto-renew is off), the server is **suspended**: it stops, and nobody can use it. You get a notice and an email.

- Every page of the server shows **Suspended — deletes in …**, a countdown to the end of the grace period your host set.
- About a day before the deletion you are warned again.
- To get the server back, top up and choose **Renew and unsuspend**. With auto-renew on, a top-up that covers the renewal also renews it by itself.
- When the countdown runs out, the server and its files are **deleted** and cannot be brought back.

Download a backup of anything you want to keep if you do not plan to renew. See [Backups and schedules](/help/backups-and-schedules).

## Pay-as-you-play: auto-sleep

On plans that offer it, a server can **sleep** when nobody is playing, and sleeping time costs less.

- Turn on **Auto-sleep** on the server's console. It shows how many minutes without players it waits, and how much cheaper sleeping time is.
- When nobody has played for that long, the server is stopped gracefully and the console says it is sleeping.
- It wakes as soon as anyone starts it: from the console, a schedule, **Wake server**, or a visitor on its [public page](/help/public-server-page) if you allow that.
- The discount is taken off the next renewal. The console shows how long the server slept this period, the tokens saved so far, and what you saved at the last renewal.

Auto-sleep works on Minecraft Java servers for now. On other games the console says it is not supported yet.
