# Friends and sharing access

Anyone can *play* on your server with its address. To let someone help *run* it, such as using the console or editing files, invite them as a user of the server.

## Friends

Open **Friends** in your account to keep a list of the people you play with.

1. Under **Add a friend**, enter their username or email and send a request.
2. They get the request in the panel and become your friend once they accept.
3. Requests you sent wait under **Sent requests** until they are accepted or you cancel them.

Friends can be picked from a list when you invite someone to a server, instead of typing their email. Removing a friend does not remove any access they already have to your servers.

## Inviting someone to a server

1. Open the server and go to **Users**.
2. Create a new user: enter their email, or pick one of your friends.
3. Choose what they may do by ticking permissions, or start from a template (below).
4. Save. They see the server on their dashboard.

You can only give permissions you hold yourself. **Select all permissions** gives them everything, so only use it for someone you fully trust.

## Permission templates

A template fills in the permission checkboxes for you; you can still change them before inviting.

- **Built-in templates:** Viewer, Moderator, Builder and Co-owner.
- **From your host:** templates your host added for everyone.
- **Your own:** tick the permissions you want and choose **Save these permissions as a template**, or manage them under **Permission Templates** in your account. Only you see your own templates.

If a template holds permissions you do not have, they are left out and the form tells you.
