# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data MonoForge collects, why, how long we keep it and what your rights are. It is written for the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP).

**Controller:** [Your name / business], [postal address]. Contact: [contact email] or our [Discord](https://discord.gg/B3fd45pgvC).

## 1. What we collect

- **Account data:** your email address, username and, if you set one, your password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash; nobody can read them.
- **Login connections:** when you sign in with or link Discord or Google, we store the ID, username, email address and avatar link that the provider returns.
- **Activity logs:** the panel records actions on your account and servers (for example sign-ins, file changes and power actions) together with the **IP address** they came from. You see your own activity log; server owners and the people they invite see the server's log. Addresses of our own infrastructure, and addresses an administrator chooses to hide, are not shown.
- **Payments:** tokens are sold by **Tebex**, who is the merchant of record and processes your card or other payment details. **The panel never sees or stores card data.** We receive and keep a record of each payment: the transaction ID, the amount and currency, the number of tokens and its status (for example completed, refunded or charged back).
- **Token history:** every change to your token balance (purchases, renewals, refunds).
- **Your servers:** the files on your servers, their backups and their console logs. We do not look at them except to run the service, to investigate abuse or when you ask us for help.
- **AI assistant:** the messages you send to the assistant, and the server information it reads to answer you, are sent to our AI provider to generate the answer, and the conversation is stored so you can see it again.
- **Email:** emails we send you (for example password resets and renewal warnings) are delivered through Resend.

## 2. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

- To provide the service you signed up for: your account, your servers, payments and support. Legal basis: the contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- To keep the panel secure and to prevent abuse and fraud, including the activity log and its IP addresses. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a safe service (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- To keep payment records as accounting and tax law requires. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
- To send emails about your account and servers. Legal basis: the contract. We do not send marketing emails.

## 3. How long we keep it

- Account data, login connections and token history: as long as your account exists.
- Activity logs: deleted automatically after the retention period set on the panel (90 days unless the operator changes it).
- Payment records: as long as accounting law requires (usually 10 years). The full notifications Tebex sends us are cut down to the transaction ID, type and date after **90 days**.
- Server files, backups and console logs: until you delete them, or until the server is deleted (for example after an unpaid renewal and its grace period).
- AI assistant conversations: until you delete them or your account is deleted.

## 4. Who processes data for us

We share data only with the services we need to run MonoForge:

- **Tebex** (payments, merchant of record);
- **Resend** (sending email);
- **Cloudflare** (network, protection against attacks, and the connection to the panel);
- **playit.gg** (tunnels that make some game servers reachable);
- **our AI provider, [AI provider name]** (the AI assistant);
- **Discord** and **Google** (only when you choose to sign in with them).

Some of these services are located outside Switzerland and the EU, for example in the United States. Where that is the case we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data.

## 5. Your rights

You have the right to **access** your data, to have it **corrected**, to have it **deleted**, to receive it in a portable format (**export**), to **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest and to restrict it. To use a right, contact us on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/B3fd45pgvC) or at [contact email]. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, and we answer within 30 days.

Some data has to be kept even after you ask for deletion, for example payment records that accounting law requires.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or the authority of the EU country where you live.

## 6. Cookies

The panel uses only the cookies it needs to work: a **session cookie** that keeps you signed in and a **CSRF cookie** that protects forms against forged requests. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. If a captcha is enabled, the captcha provider (Google reCAPTCHA or Cloudflare Turnstile) may set its own cookies on the sign-in pages.

## 7. Changes

When we change this policy in an important way, the panel asks you to accept the new version.
