# Refund Policy

This policy explains when you can get your money back for tokens bought on MonoForge. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Who handles payments

Tokens are sold through **Tebex**, our payment partner and the merchant of record. Tebex takes the payment and, when a refund is approved, sends the money back to the payment method you used.

## 2. Unused tokens

You can ask for a refund of tokens you have **not spent** within **14 days of the purchase**. Contact us on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/B3fd45pgvC) or at [contact email] with the transaction ID from your Tebex receipt. When the refund is approved, the refunded tokens are removed from your balance.

## 3. Spent tokens and running servers

Tokens that were already spent, for example on a server or its renewal, and the server period that is running are **not refundable**, except where the law of your country requires a refund. By buying a server you ask us to start the service straight away.

If a server cannot be used for a long time because of a problem on our side, contact us. We may give you tokens back for that time.

## 4. Chargebacks

If you dispute a payment with your bank or card provider (a chargeback) instead of asking us, the tokens from that payment are **taken back** from your balance, even if this makes your balance negative, and your account may be **suspended** until the matter is settled. Please talk to us first; we are happy to help.

## 5. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country.
