# Refund Policy

This policy explains what happens with your money and tokens after you buy them on MonoForge. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Who handles payments

Tokens are sold through **Tebex**, our payment partner and the merchant of record. Tebex takes the payment and its own terms apply to it.

## 2. All purchases are final

**Token purchases are non-refundable.** Once you have bought tokens, we do not exchange them back for money, whether you have spent them or not.

## 3. Your tokens never expire

In return, **your tokens are yours to keep**. Tokens you have bought **never expire** and stay in your wallet for as long as your account exists, so you can use them whenever you like: today, next month or next year.

## 4. Spent tokens and running servers

Tokens spent on a server or its renewal, and the server period that is running, are not refundable either. By buying a server you ask us to start the service straight away.

If a server cannot be used for a long time because of a problem on our side, contact us on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/B3fd45pgvC) or at [contact email]. We may give you **tokens back** for that time.

## 5. Mistakes and problems

If something went wrong with a purchase, for example you were charged but did not receive your tokens, contact us with the transaction ID from your Tebex receipt and we will sort it out.

## 6. Chargebacks

If you dispute a payment with your bank or card provider (a chargeback) instead of talking to us, the tokens from that payment are **taken back** from your balance, even if this makes your balance negative, and your account may be **suspended** until the matter is settled. Please talk to us first; we are happy to help.

## 7. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country. Where the law gives you a right to a refund, that right applies.
